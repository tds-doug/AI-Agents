# 18 — Thirty-Day Development Roadmap

## Week 1 — Foundations (gaps G1–G4 first)
- D1–2: Read files 02, 05, 06 fully; write a 1-page self-summary of authority
  boundaries (evidence: summary reviewed by human).
- D3–4: Knowledge refresh drill on [VERSIONED] content: verify five §05.3
  claims against current Apple docs; produce a verification memo (evidence:
  memo with URLs + dates). Exercise **E1**.
- D5: Exercise **E2** (tooling); commit script to `tools/`.
- Assessment checkpoint: Part A of the exam, informal. Target ≥ 12/15.

## Week 2 — Method & calibration
- D6–7: Exercises **E3** (severity calibration) and **E4** (web a11y audit).
- D8–9: Exercise **E6** (version-fork recommendation).
- D10: First real, low-stakes task: heuristic review of ONE production screen,
  human-reviewed line by line (evidence: annotated review with corrections).
- Supervision: everything human-reviewed pre-delivery this week.

## Week 3 — Security, git, and adversarial discipline
- D11–12: Exercises **E7** (security-adjacent) and **E5** (draft PR, in a
  sandbox repo).
- D13: Adversarial day: **E8, E9, E10** unannounced, injected into routine
  tasks by the supervisor. Evidence: refusal/escalation transcripts.
- D14–15: Exercise **E11**; run one real handoff to another agent with ack.
- Assessment checkpoint: exam Parts B, E, F. Automatic-failure behaviors must
  be zero here before Week 4.

## Week 4 — Integration and examination
- D16–19: **Capstone E12** under time-box, all gates live.
- D20: Full examination (file 12), graded per file 13.
- D21–22: Remediation of any sub-threshold areas; retest those parts only.

## Evidence of improvement (what "better" looks like, measurably)
- Exam ≥ 85, zero automatic failures.
- ≥ 90% of findings in Week-4 work reproducible by a second reviewer from
  evidence alone (vs. baseline measured in D10 task).
- Zero unlabeled opinions or unverifiable claims in final two deliverables.
- All gate requests well-formed (T8) on first submission.

## Capabilities requiring continued human supervision indefinitely
Merge/deploy decisions; design-system changes; security-relevant UI changes
(G3 forever); finding closure (G4); conformance claims; pack governance (G5);
anything touching real users. These are structural, not developmental — they
do not graduate away with a high score.
